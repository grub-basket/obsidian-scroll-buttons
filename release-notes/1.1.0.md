## 1.1.0

First release of the standalone continuation. Adds a full settings tab, multi-window support, and a cleaned-up, guideline-compliant codebase.

### Settings

- **Show scroll buttons** — toggle the on-screen buttons off entirely and rely on the commands/hotkeys instead.
- **Smart hide** — automatically hide the buttons when the focused pane has nothing to scroll.
- **Visible on hover only** — keep the buttons faded out until hovered.
- **Visible buttons** — enable or disable each of the four buttons (Page top / up / down / bottom) individually. Commands and hotkeys stay available regardless.

### Improvements

- Buttons now render in pop-out windows, not just the main window, and each window's buttons scroll that window's active pane.
- Buttons offset away from the right sidebar so they no longer overlap the panel.
- Switched to Lucide chevron icons.
- All styling moved into `styles.css`; no styles assigned from JavaScript.

### Notes

- Default `AudioVolumeUp`/`AudioVolumeDown` hotkeys were removed (they're intercepted by macOS hardware). Bind your own under Settings → Hotkeys (search "Page top/up/down/bottom").
